#  Types of Cryptocurrencies

The cryptocurrency ecosystem consists of a **diverse range of digital assets**, each serving distinct roles—from store of value and utility to governance and programmable finance.

GSHI's strategy involves understanding, integrating, and optimizing these categories to provide **multi-asset services** across DeFi, CeFi, and DAO infrastructures.

##  Major Categories of Cryptocurrencies

| Type              | Purpose                                          | Examples                           |
|-------------------|--------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------|
| **Store of Value**| Long-term value retention, inflation hedge       | BTC, LTC                           |
| **Stablecoins**   | Pegged to fiat or assets for low volatility      | USDC, DAI, USDT, GHO                |
| **Utility Tokens**| Used to pay for protocol functions or services   | ETH, BNB, MATIC, AVAX              |
| **Governance Tokens** | Voting rights in decentralized platforms   | UNI, AAVE, COMP                    |
| **Security Tokens**| Represent traditional securities or shares      | Synthetix, tZERO                   |
| **Payment Tokens**| Designed for fast transactions                   | XRP, XLM, DASH                     |
| **NFTs**          | Unique, non-fungible assets (identity, art, IP) | BAYC, ENS, RWA NFTs                |
| **Wrapped Assets**| Tokenized versions of other assets               | WBTC (Wrapped BTC), renBTC         |

---

##  1. Store of Value (SoV)

- **Purpose**: Preserve wealth over time
- **Examples**: Bitcoin (BTC), Litecoin (LTC)
- Often compared to digital gold
- Limited supply + scarcity models

---

##  2. Stablecoins

- Pegged to USD or other assets
- Types:
  - **Fiat-backed** (USDC, USDT)
  - **Crypto-collateralized** (DAI, GHO)
  - **Algorithmic** (FRAX, UST - failed model)
- Used for lending, trading, yield farming

> “Stablecoins are the digital rails of modern crypto finance.”

---

##  3. Utility Tokens

- Fuel protocol operations and fees
- Examples: ETH for gas, MATIC for L2 fees
- Often burned to reduce supply

---

##  4. Governance Tokens

- Used for voting in DAOs
- Allocate treasury, decide upgrades
- Examples: UNI (Uniswap), AAVE, MKR

---

##  5. Security Tokens

- Represent regulated assets (shares, debt)
- Often used in tokenized real estate or equity offerings
- Subject to SEC-style oversight

---

##  6. Payment Tokens

- Designed for high-speed, low-fee transactions
- Ripple (XRP): cross-border payments
- Stellar (XLM): tokenizing money movement

---

##  7. Non-Fungible Tokens (NFTs)

- Unique identifiers of ownership or access
- Use cases:
  - Art
  - Identity & credentials
  - Tokenized real estate (RWA NFTs)

---

##  8. Wrapped Assets

- Tokenized representations of non-native tokens
- Enable cross-chain use (e.g., BTC on Ethereum)
- Examples:
  - WBTC (Wrapped BTC)
  - renBTC
  - stETH (liquid staked ETH)
