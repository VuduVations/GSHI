#  Common Trading Strategies Encountered in the Cryptocurrency Market

The cryptocurrency market is fertile ground for innovative trading approaches, many of which derive from **traditional finance** but are adapted for a **faster, decentralized, and more volatile** environment.

> “Speed, data, and strategy shape success in crypto.”

---

##  1. Momentum-Based (Trend Following)

- **Goal**: Profit from continuation of existing trends
- **Description**: Exploits strong directional moves in the market
- **Used When**: Speed and market confirmation matter
- **Example**: Buy on confirmed uptrend; sell on breakdown

---

##  2. Value Investing

- **Goal**: Buy undervalued assets based on long-term reversion to mean
- **Contrast**: Momentum follows trends; value investing anticipates mean reversion
- **Outlook**: Long-term
- **Challenge**: Requires patience and conviction during downturns

---

##  3. Momentum Investing

- **Goal**: Systematically profit from delayed investor reactions
- **Method**: Chase performance by capitalizing on herd behavior
- **Unique Edge**: Exploits emotional inefficiency in human trading

---

##  4. Earnings Momentum Strategies

- **Goal**: Leverage underreaction to earnings-related news
- **Trigger**: Positive earnings surprise not yet priced in
- **Application**: Altcoins with revenue-generating protocols (e.g., RWA, DeFi tokens)

---

##  5. Price Momentum Strategies

- **Goal**: Trade based on longer-term profitability signals
- **Mechanism**: Profit from lag in full market realization of broader data
- **Signal Source**: Market depth, address growth, TVL, oracles

---

##  6. Arbitrage Strategies

- **Goal**: Exploit price discrepancies across venues
- **Types**:
  - Cross-exchange arbitrage (e.g., BTC cheaper on Exchange A vs. B)
  - Pairs arbitrage (BTC and DOGE correlation logic)
- **Example**: BTC falls, DOGE hasn’t—short DOGE before it follows

---

##  7. Statistical Arbitrage

- **Goal**: Short-duration trades based on statistical patterns
- **Duration**: Seconds to a few days
- **Tools**: Mean reversion, correlation analysis
- **Used by**: High-frequency traders, quant hedge funds

---

##  8. Automated Market Making Strategies (AMMs)

- **Goal**: Provide liquidity on DEXs and earn fees
- **Function**: Uses smart contracts and math, not order books
- **Examples**: Uniswap, Curve, Balancer
- **Edge**: Non-custodial and composable

> “AMMs democratize market making—anyone can be a liquidity provider.”

---

##  9. Backtesting as a Core Component

- **Definition**: Simulating algorithm performance using historical data
- **Purpose**: Optimize parameters without risking real capital
- **Benefit**: Validate and refine before deploying live strategies

---
