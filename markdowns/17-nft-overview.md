#  Non-Fungible Tokens (NFTs): Overview, Value, and Use Cases

Non-Fungible Tokens (NFTs) represent **unique digital or tokenized assets**. Unlike fungible cryptocurrencies like BTC or ETH, each NFT is **individually distinguishable** and cannot be exchanged on a one-to-one basis with another token.

---

##  What Is an NFT?

> “Non-fungible” means an asset is **one-of-a-kind** and **not interchangeable** with anything else of equal value.

- Each NFT contains **metadata** and **ownership information**
- They serve as **proof of authenticity and ownership**
- Built on **blockchain standards** like ERC-721 and ERC-1155

Examples:
- Artwork
- Music
- Domain names
- Gaming assets
- Tokenized real-world property

##  How NFTs Get Their Value

1. **Scarcity** – limited supply increases perceived worth
2. **Authenticity** – verifiable history and creator
3. **Utility** – access, voting, staking, game functionality
4. **Community & Hype** – social sentiment drives market demand
5. **Cultural Significance** – memes, moments, and milestones

> “Like fiat currency, NFTs gain value from collective belief.”
