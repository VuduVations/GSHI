#  Altcoins – Beyond Bitcoin

The term **"altcoin"** refers to any cryptocurrency that is not Bitcoin. Altcoins make up the vast majority of digital assets and represent a wide range of use cases, technological innovations, and governance models.

> “Altcoins are where experimentation meets decentralization.”

---

##  What Are Altcoins?

- The term originates from “alternative to Bitcoin.”
- Includes coins and tokens on a variety of blockchains (Ethereum, Solana, Avalanche, etc.)
- Altcoins range from large-cap ecosystems to micro-cap speculative projects

##  Categories of Altcoins

Many altcoins fall into one or more of the following categories:

| Category            | Description                                      | Examples                            |
|---------------------|--------------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------|
| **Utility Tokens**  | Fuel protocol usage or services                  | ETH, MATIC, FTM                      |
| **Governance Tokens**| Voting rights in DAOs                          | UNI, AAVE, MKR                       |
| **Exchange Tokens** | Power centralized platforms                      | BNB (Binance), HT (Huobi), OKB      |
| **Privacy Coins**   | Obfuscate transaction details                    | XMR (Monero), ZEC (Zcash)            |
| **Smart Contract Platforms** | Host dApps & DeFi                     | ADA (Cardano), SOL, AVAX            |
| **Layer 2 / Scaling Tokens** | Support rollups or sidechains         | OP (Optimism), ARB (Arbitrum)        |
| **Gaming/Metaverse**| Support virtual economies and NFT ecosystems     | SAND, MANA, AXS                      |

---

##  Why Altcoins Matter

- Lower barriers to innovation compared to Bitcoin
- Higher volatility = higher upside (and downside)
- Drive specialization in infrastructure, privacy, payments, and more
- Can outperform BTC in **bull cycles** and offer diverse yield strategies

---

##  Altcoin Market Landscape

- Bitcoin dominance: ~40–50% of total crypto market cap
- Ethereum + top altcoins: ~50–60%
- Thousands of altcoins in circulation—most with low liquidity or weak fundamentals
