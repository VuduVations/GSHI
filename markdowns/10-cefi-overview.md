#  Centralized Finance (CeFi)

Centralized Finance (CeFi) refers to crypto platforms that operate under **traditional financial structures**, providing user-friendly access to digital assets while maintaining **centralized control, custody, and compliance**.

> "CeFi bridges the gap between the crypto-native world and mainstream adoption."

---

##  What Is CeFi?

In CeFi systems:
- Users deposit funds into a centralized entity
- The platform holds custody of funds (similar to a bank)
- Orders, trades, and withdrawals are controlled off-chain
- Regulatory frameworks guide operations (KYC, AML, custody rules)

---

##  CeFi Functional Layers

| Layer              | Description                                   |
|--------------------|-----------------------------------------------|
| **Custody**         | Platform controls wallet keys                 |
| **Trading Engine**  | Orders processed off-chain                    |
| **Compliance**      | AML/KYC verification + geographic restrictions|
| **Risk Management** | Insurance pools, collateralized loans        |
| **UX Layer**        | Simplified onboarding + customer support      |

---

##  Advantages of CeFi

 **User-friendly** for beginners  
 **Fiat on/off ramps** (USD, EUR, etc.)  
 **Account recovery options**  
 **Insurance and regulatory reporting**  
 **Customer service and dispute resolution**

---

##  Limitations and Risks

- **Custodial risk** (loss of private keys = loss of control)
- **Censorship risk** (freezing of accounts, denied withdrawals)
- **Lack of transparency** (off-chain order books)
- **KYC friction** (for users in restricted regions)
- **Potential for insolvency** (see: Celsius, FTX failures)
