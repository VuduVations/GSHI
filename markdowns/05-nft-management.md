#  NFT Management

Non-Fungible Tokens (NFTs) represent a new class of digital assets that are **unique, indivisible, and irreplaceable**. Unlike fungible cryptocurrencies (e.g., BTC, ETH), NFTs are designed to certify digital ownership and authenticity.

---

##  What Is an NFT?

An **NFT** is a unit of data stored on a blockchain that verifies ownership of a unique digital item. This could include:

- Art & collectibles
- Music and media
- Gaming assets
- Identity and credentials
- Tokenized real-world assets

> “An NFT is not interchangeable or duplicable—each is one-of-a-kind.”

##  Functional Uses for GSHI

As a decentralized financial platform, **GSHI can provide the following NFT-related services**:

### 1. NFT Custody & Security
- Secure on-chain/off-chain storage
- Multisig and custodial options
- Recovery support and risk mitigation

### 2. Marketplace Integration
- Support for user-created and curated marketplaces
- Tokenized assets as collateral or investment classes
- Support for ERC-721, ERC-1155, and future standards

### 3. Fractionalization
- Turn a single NFT into many tradable ERC-20 tokens
- Enables collective ownership and liquidity

### 4. NFT Staking
- Lock NFTs in smart contracts for rewards or governance rights
- Power NFT-based DAOs or community engagement models
