# GSHI – Decentralized Banking Feasibility Analysis

Welcome to the official documentation and strategic feasibility study of **GSHI** — a decentralized-first, yield-optimized, compliance-ready crypto banking initiative conducted by VuduVations, Lead S. Halverson.

---

> **Historical Context Notice**  
> This feasibility analysis was originally developed between 2020 and 2021 to anticipate the evolution of decentralized finance, DAO governance, algorithmic trading, crypto banking services, and regulatory adaptation.  
>  
> While certain tactical elements (such as token rankings and specific market participants) may have evolved, the core strategic architectures, risk models, and governance frameworks outlined herein remain highly relevant in 2025.  
>  
> This publication reflects early strategic foresight and foundational analysis of emerging crypto-native financial structures.
